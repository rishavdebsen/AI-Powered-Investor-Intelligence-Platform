# Aurora Retail Group FY2025 Annual Report (fictional sample)

## Business Overview

Aurora Retail Group is a fictional online retailer of home goods and
everyday essentials, serving customers in India and the Gulf region. This
document is a small synthetic sample written for testing and evaluation; it
describes no real company and contains no real financial data.

## Financial Highlights

Total revenue of $7.6 billion for fiscal year 2025. Revenue grew 18% year
over year, driven by same-day delivery adoption in metro cities. Operating
income was $420 million, and operating margin was 5.5%. Net income was
$305 million. Diluted earnings per share was $1.92, up from $1.41 in the
prior year. Operating cash flow was $690 million. Total assets stood at
$5.2 billion, and total liabilities stood at $3.1 billion.

## Segment Results

The Marketplace segment generated $5.9 billion in revenue, while Private
Labels contributed $1.2 billion. The remaining $0.5 billion came from
Logistics Services offered to third-party sellers.

## Growth Drivers

Same-day delivery coverage expanded from 12 to 27 cities, lifting order
frequency among existing customers. Private-label penetration grew as the
company added mid-price home essentials, and advertising revenue from
marketplace sellers became a meaningful high-margin stream.

## Risk Factors

Intense price competition compresses margins, and delivery costs are
sensitive to fuel prices. The company carries seasonal inventory risk ahead
of festive sales periods, and seller concentration means the top 200 sellers
generate a large share of marketplace volume.

## Outlook

Management expects fiscal 2026 revenue growth of 15-17% and plans to open
four new fulfilment centres. The company intends to pilot a subscription
delivery programme in two metro cities.
