# Vertex Mobility FY2025 Annual Report (fictional sample)

## Business Overview

Vertex Mobility is a fictional manufacturer of electric two-wheelers and
fleet-charging systems, headquartered in Bengaluru, India. This document is
a small synthetic sample written for testing and evaluation; it describes
no real company and contains no real financial data.

## Financial Highlights

Total revenue of $1.9 billion for fiscal year 2025. Revenue grew 34% year
over year, driven by fleet-electrification contracts with logistics
operators. Operating income was $150 million, and operating margin was 7.9%.
Net income was $96 million. Diluted earnings per share was $0.58, up from
$0.31 in the prior year. Operating cash flow was $210 million. Total assets
stood at $2.7 billion, and total liabilities stood at $1.8 billion.

## Segment Results

The Vehicles segment generated $1.4 billion in revenue, while Charging
Infrastructure contributed $0.4 billion. The remaining $0.1 billion came
from Fleet Software subscriptions.

## Growth Drivers

Fleet-electrification contracts with national logistics operators scaled
rapidly as fuel-cost savings became easier to prove. The company's battery
swapping pilots reduced charging downtime for delivery fleets, and falling
cell prices improved vehicle gross margins through the year.

## Risk Factors

Battery-cell supply is concentrated among a few overseas suppliers, and
import duties can move costs quickly. Government incentive programmes for
electric vehicles may change, and larger incumbents are entering the
electric two-wheeler market with aggressive pricing.

## Outlook

Management expects fiscal 2026 revenue growth of 25-30% and plans to
commission a second assembly plant. The company intends to expand battery
swapping to five additional cities.
