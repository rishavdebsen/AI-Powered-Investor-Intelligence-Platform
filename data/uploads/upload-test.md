# Meridian Industries FY2025 Annual Report v2 (fictional sample)

## Business Overview

Meridian Industries is a fictional manufacturer of industrial sensors and
automation equipment, headquartered in Pune, India. The company sells to
automotive, energy and logistics customers across 14 countries. This document
is a small synthetic sample written for testing and evaluation; it describes
no real company and contains no real financial data.

## Financial Highlights

Total revenue of $4.2 billion for fiscal year 2025. Revenue grew 11% year
over year, driven by demand for factory-automation sensors. Operating income
was $980 million, and operating margin was 23%. Net income was $742 million.
Diluted earnings per share was $5.16, up from $4.61 in the prior year.

Free cash flow of $610 million supported continued investment in the
company's sensor research programme and two capacity expansions.

## Segment Results

The Industrial Automation segment generated $2.6 billion in revenue, while
Energy Systems contributed $1.1 billion. The remaining $0.5 billion came from
Logistics Solutions, which the company treats as an emerging segment.

## Risk Factors

The company depends on a small number of semiconductor suppliers, and
extended lead times could delay shipments. Currency movements affect reported
results because 62% of revenue is billed outside India. A downturn in
automotive capital spending would reduce demand for automation equipment.

## Outlook

Management expects fiscal 2026 revenue growth of 8-10% and plans to expand
the Pune facility. The company intends to publish segment-level margin
reporting starting in the first quarter of fiscal 2026.
